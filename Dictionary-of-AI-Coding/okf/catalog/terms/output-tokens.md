# Overview

Output tokens are the tokens a [model](./model.md) generates, including prose, code, [tool calls](./tool-call.md), and reasoning output that may not be displayed by the harness. They are counted for each [model provider request](./model-provider-request.md).

# Operational implication

Generation happens incrementally, so large outputs influence both cost and perceived [turn](./turn.md) latency. Focused edits can avoid spending output on unnecessary whole-file rewrites.
