Parts assurance process linking EEE part selection, screening, qualification, and derating to radiation assurance evidence.

# Procedure
1. Identify the mission radiation environment and applicable standards.
2. Derive part-level TID, SEE, and DDD requirements with margin and shielding assumptions.
3. Select candidate EEE parts and identify existing qualification, screening, derating, and radiation data.
4. Plan focused ground testing when existing evidence is insufficient for the application.
5. Evaluate test results against mission needs, document residual risk, and record mitigations or waivers.

# TI handbook parts-selection augmentation
- The handbook cautions that commercial flows, process transfers, lot-to-lot variation, grade substitutions, die shrinks, package changes, and date-code assumptions can invalidate radiation-data reuse.
- It recommends evaluating whether the tested part is exactly the same as the intended part and whether operating conditions, setup, and failure criteria match the application.
- For power products, derating and application-specific capacitors/load conditions can be essential to avoid confusing setup artifacts with radiation effects.
