# Assurance Workflow

* [Displacement Damage Dose (DDD)](displacement-damage-dose.md) - Non-ionizing damage from particle displacement in material lattices that can degrade optoelectronic, semiconductor, and detector performance.
* [Evidence and Provenance Model](evidence-and-provenance.md) - Catalog convention for retaining immutable source Markdown evidence and connecting derived concepts to cited source-document concepts.
* [Mission Profile Level Derivation](mission-profile-level-derivation.md) - Process for converting mission orbit, duration, shielding, and environment assumptions into TID, SEE, and DDD evaluation levels.
* [NASA GSFC Compendium of Radiation Effects Test Results](compendium.md) - Retained Markdown evidence for NASA GSFC Compendium of Radiation Effects Test Results.
* [NASA GSFC Radiation Effects Test Results](nasa-gsfc-radiation-effects-test-results.md) - NEPP/GSFC evidence collection of radiation effects test results and techniques used to interpret application-specific device behavior.
* [Parts Selection Screening Qualification and Derating](parts-selection-screening-qualification-derating.md) - Parts assurance process linking EEE part selection, screening, qualification, and derating to radiation assurance evidence.
* [Published Radiation Test Data Applicability](published-radiation-test-data-applicability.md) - Review workflow for deciding whether published radiation reports and vendor data can support a mission-specific part-use decision.
* [Radiation Hardening and Mitigation Techniques](radiation-hardening-and-mitigation-techniques.md) - Workflow concept for selecting process, layout, circuit, shielding, and system-level mitigations against electronics radiation effects.
* [Radiation Hardness Assurance Workflow](rha-assurance-workflow.md) - End-to-end workflow for identifying mission radiation risks, deriving levels, selecting tests, evaluating parts, documenting margins, and managing residual risk.
* [Semiconductor Technology Radiation Sensitivity](semiconductor-technology-radiation-sensitivity.md) - Device-technology guidance for assessing how CMOS, bipolar, BiCMOS, SOI, power, memory, and analog products respond differently to TID, SEE, DDD, and dose-rate effects.
* [Single Event Effects (SEE)](single-event-effects.md) - Prompt effects caused by a single energetic particle interaction, including destructive and non-destructive event modes.
* [Space Radiation Environment](space-radiation-environment.md) - Mission radiation environment consisting of trapped particles, solar particle events, galactic cosmic rays, and shielding-dependent transport to parts.
* [Total Ionizing Dose (TID)](total-ionizing-dose.md) - Cumulative ionizing radiation effect that can shift device parameters and degrade electronic function over mission life.
