ANSTO's provider-authored page presents a multi-facility radiation-testing service spanning charged-particle, cobalt-60 gamma, and synchrotron X-ray irradiation.

# Facility scope
- **Centre for Accelerator Science (CAS):** proton and heavier-ion beams for single-event-effect testing, plus proton beams for displacement-damage testing.
- **Gamma Technology Research Irradiator (GATRI):** cobalt-60 gamma irradiation for total-ionizing-dose testing over a large planar field.
- **Gammacell 220:** cobalt-60 gamma irradiation within a cylindrical sample volume for total-ionizing-dose testing.
- **Australian Synchrotron:** monochromatic X-ray irradiation presented for total-ionizing-dose testing, pre-screening, sensitive-area mapping, and laminography.
