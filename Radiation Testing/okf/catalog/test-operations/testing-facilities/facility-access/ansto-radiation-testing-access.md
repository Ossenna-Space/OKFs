The retained ANSTO page directs prospective users to facility personnel and ANSTO contact pathways rather than documenting a complete proposal, scheduling, pricing, or user-access process.

# Provider-listed pathways
- Centre for Accelerator Science irradiation enquiries are associated with the CAS Irradiations Team Lead.
- Industry enquiries are associated with a Commercial Technical Consultant.
- The page identifies ANSTO sites at Lucas Heights, New South Wales, and Clayton, Victoria; its facility descriptions associate CAS and cobalt-60 services with ANSTO operations and the X-ray capability with the Australian Synchrotron.
- The retained capture contains contact labels and telephone details, but its web links resolve only to `#`; use ANSTO's current official contact channels to verify personnel and routing.

# Information to confirm before booking
- Current beam/source availability, supported species and energies, dose or flux range, geometry, and sample constraints.
- Access route, proposal or commercial process, lead time, pricing, site induction, export or security constraints, and test-support responsibilities.
- Required fixtures, cables, monitoring equipment, dosimetry, calibration evidence, data deliverables, reporting, and applicable safety controls.

# Access limitation
The retained page is not an access agreement or current quotation. Contact names, roles, availability, and procedures may change and require direct confirmation.
