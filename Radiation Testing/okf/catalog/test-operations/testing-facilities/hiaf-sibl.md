HIAF-SIBL is the Space Irradiation Beamline at the Australian National University Heavy Ion Accelerator Facility in Canberra. The provider brochure describes it as dedicated to charged-particle radiation testing for space and as the highest-energy heavy-ion space radiation-testing facility in Australia.

# Facility context
- HIAF operates a 15 MV 14UD tandem Van de Graaff accelerator coupled to a 6 MV-equivalent superconducting linac booster.
- The brochure says HIAF can deliver beams of nearly any element to 11 beamlines, with one beamline dedicated to radiation testing.
- The brochure states facility operation of 3,500–5,000 hours per year on a 24/7 basis.
- HIAF-SIBL offers consultation and dedicated physicist/technical support for client-defined tests.
