The retained ANSTO page is a provider-authored facility capability statement. It describes irradiation sources, geometries, technical features, contacts, and potential applications; it does not state that ANSTO testing automatically confers formal qualification, certification, standards compliance, or mission acceptance.

# Service boundary
- ANSTO may provide irradiation infrastructure and facility support within a separately agreed scope.
- The retained page does not identify a complete standards-controlled test method, accreditation scope, formal certification service, or mission acceptance authority.
- References to SEE, DDD, TID, and pre-screening describe intended applications and available sources, not proof that a campaign will satisfy a particular standard or program.
- X-ray pre-screening and sensitive-area mapping are screening or diagnostic activities unless a governing assurance plan explicitly accepts them for another purpose.

# Test-owner responsibilities
The test owner and responsible assurance authority must separately define and approve the governing standards, mission-derived levels and margins, test article identity and configuration, bias and operating conditions, dosimetry and uncertainty controls, acceptance criteria, anomaly handling, reporting, evidence applicability, and final qualification or mission-acceptance decision.

# Evidence interpretation
Facility records can support an assurance decision when provenance, configuration, methods, uncertainties, and applicability are documented. They do not replace the responsible organization's qualification, certification, or mission-acceptance process.
