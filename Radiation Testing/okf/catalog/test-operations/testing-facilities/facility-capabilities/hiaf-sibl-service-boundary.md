The HIAF-SIBL brochure explicitly states that the facility **does not provide formal radiation certification**. It offers irradiation, consultation, experiment support, and evidence intended to help users understand device or shielding-material performance in a space-like radiation environment.

# Assurance interpretation
- Facility access and a completed irradiation campaign do not by themselves establish qualification, certification, or mission acceptance.
- The brochure is a capability statement, not a governing test standard.
- The test owner remains responsible for selecting applicable standards, deriving mission-specific conditions and acceptance criteria, controlling the test article and configuration, documenting dosimetry and uncertainty, interpreting results, and obtaining any required independent approval or certification.
- Facility-generated results can become evidence in the [RHA assurance workflow](../../../assurance-workflow/rha-assurance-workflow.md) when their applicability and provenance are documented.
