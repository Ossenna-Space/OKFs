The retained ANSTO page reports the following technical capabilities. Values are provider claims and should be reconfirmed for a proposed campaign.

# SEE ion beams — Centre for Accelerator Science
- Protons: up to 15 MeV; stated LET in silicon 0.02–0.2 MeV·cm²/mg; stated range in silicon 15–2000 µm.
- Heavier ions: up to approximately 120 MeV depending on ion; stated LET in silicon 0.2–45 MeV·cm²/mg; stated range in silicon 1–100 µm.
- Stated flux: 10⁴–10¹² particles/(cm²·s); low-rate regime 50 Hz–50 kHz; high-current regime pA to µA.
- Vacuum: microbeam spot down to 1 µm, scanning or single-spot irradiation, scan area up to 3 × 3 mm, decapsulated components, chamber approximately 200 mm internal diameter × 100 mm high, and real-time measurement cabling.
- Ambient air: microbeam spot down to 10 µm, defocused single spot, scan area up to 3 × 3 mm, sample/hybrid scanning up to 50 × 50 mm, bare components only, enclosure 280 × 380 × 230 mm, and real-time measurement cabling.

# DDD proton beams — Centre for Accelerator Science
- Protons up to 15 MeV, stated range in silicon up to 2000 µm, and stated flux 10⁴–10⁹ particles/(cm²·s).
- Vacuum and ambient-air geometry broadly follows the proton arrangements above; the page lists 1 µm and 10 µm minimum microbeam spots respectively.

# TID cobalt-60 — GATRI and Gammacell 220
- **GATRI:** cobalt-60 gamma energies 1.17 and 1.33 MeV; stated dose rate approximately 0.01–0.22 Gy(Si)/s (1–22 rad(Si)/s); uniform planar field 1 × 1 m; components, boards, and larger systems; selected-area shielding; room greater than 20 m³; real-time measurement cabling.
- **Gammacell 220:** cobalt-60 gamma energies 1.17 and 1.33 MeV; stated dose rate approximately 0.12 Gy(Si)/s (12 rad(Si)/s); cylindrical field 15.2 cm diameter × 20.6 cm high; components and fitting boards; real-time measurement cabling.

# TID and pre-screening X-rays — Australian Synchrotron
- Superconducting Multipole Wiggler on a 3 GeV electron storage ring.
- Monochromatic 20–120 keV X-rays and stated flux 10⁷–10¹⁴ photons/(cm²·s), depending on energy.
- The page's dose-rate expression is internally ambiguous: `~ Gy(Si)/s to kGy(Si)/s` is followed by a parenthetical `~tens of mrad(Si)/s to rad(Si)/s`; confirm the intended numerical range and units with ANSTO before use.
- Collimated beam; stated irradiated area from 100 × 100 µm to 25 × 100 mm; encapsulated and bare components; sensitive-area mapping, pre-screening, real-time measurement cabling, and laminography.

# Planning limits
These specifications do not establish a standards-compliant test method, calibration traceability, uncertainty budget, dose/fluence uniformity, device bias and temperature conditions, acceptance criteria, qualification status, or mission suitability. Capture those separately in the test plan and facility agreement.
